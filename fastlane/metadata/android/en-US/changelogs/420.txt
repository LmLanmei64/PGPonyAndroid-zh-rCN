PGPony 4.2.0 adds the ML-KEM 1024 hybrid suite beside 768, multiple identities per key, subkey display and creation, per-key fallback decryption keys and signing defaults, and reorganized Settings. Files and bundles of any size now stream without crashes. Deleting keys and clearing data are heavily safeguarded. If you generated a 768 key before 4.2.0, the app will suggest regenerating it for gpg compatibility. Mail clients without v6 support need a classical signing default. iOS parity lands in 8.2.0.
